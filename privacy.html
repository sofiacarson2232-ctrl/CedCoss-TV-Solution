Key Privacy Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or email addresses to third-party marketing entities under any circumstances.

1. Information We Collect

We only gather information necessary to provide you with secure streaming access, custom credentials, and setup assistance:

  • Contact Details: Your full name and email address when you request an entertainment pass or submit an assistance inquiry.
  • Device & Hardware Info: Primary streaming hardware (e.g. Amazon Fire TV, Smart TV model, Android device, or Apple TV) so our specialists can provide exact application parameters.
  • Technical Usage Data: Standard internet log data, IP addresses, browser types, and approximate geographic region utilized strictly for service optimization and server load-balancing.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is applied exclusively toward:

  • Generating and dispatching your streaming server credentials and device setup instructions.
  • Providing concierge assistance and answering inquiries.
  • Notifying you about critical system updates, server maintenance schedules, or pass renewal notices.
  • Detecting and mitigating fraud, service abuse, or malicious cyber activity.

3. Data Protection & Security Standards

We implement industry-standard 256-bit Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption across all web interactions. All digital communications containing credentials or customer requests are stored within secured database environments protected by strict firewall rules and role-based access controls.

4. Cookies & Web Analytics

We utilize essential and performance cookies to maintain user session preferences, analyze traffic flow, and ensure smooth website navigation. You may adjust your browser settings to decline cookies; however, some interactive features (such as dynamic plan selection) may be affected.
