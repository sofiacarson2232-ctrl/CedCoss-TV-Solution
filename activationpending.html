Helpful Questions & Answers

1. How long does device verification take?

Most pairing sessions are confirmed within 5 to 10 minutes. Once verified, your TV screen will automatically refresh and display your channels.

2. What should I do while waiting?

Keep your TV turned on with the streaming app open. If your screen dims or goes to sleep, simply press any button on your TV remote to keep it awake.

This quick check protects your account from unauthorized screen sharing and ensures your pass is linked securely to your preferred TV or media device.
