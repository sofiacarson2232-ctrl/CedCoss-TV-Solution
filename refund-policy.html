Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

Independent Service Notice: We are an independent service provider and are not affiliated with any of the brands. CedCoss TV Solution provides digital streaming subscription management and device configuration assistance.

At CedCoss TV Solution, we are committed to delivering reliable streaming experiences and transparent customer service. We want you to feel completely confident when choosing one of our entertainment passes.

7-Day Customer Satisfaction Guarantee: If our assistance concierge is unable to successfully establish your streaming service on a compatible device within the first 7 days of purchase, you are eligible for a 100% refund.

1. Qualifying Refund Circumstances

A full refund will be granted under the following conditions:

  • Technical Non-Resolution: You followed our setup guides and our concierge assistance team was unable to resolve streaming playback or credential errors on your compatible device within 7 days of pass activation.
  • Prolonged System Outage: Severe platform unavailability on our end lasting continuously for more than 48 consecutive hours.
  • Accidental Duplicate Purchase: If you were accidentally charged twice for the identical pass duration.

2. Non-Qualifying Situations

Refunds will not be approved under the following conditions:

  • Requests submitted after the 7-day satisfaction guarantee window has elapsed.
  • Local internet bandwidth limitations below the minimum recommended speed (15 Mbps for HD, 25 Mbps for 4K) as tested via third-party speed test tools.
  • Violations of our Terms of Service (such as sharing login parameters across unauthorized households beyond allowed screens or attempting unauthorized restreaming).
  • Refusal to cooperate with basic troubleshooting steps (such as entering provided credentials or installing recommended media player apps).

3. How to Request a Refund

Initiating a refund request is straightforward. Please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to info@cedcoss.online with the subject line "Refund Request - [Your Full Name]".
  2. Include your registered email address, date of purchase, and a brief description of the difficulty experienced.
  3. Our billing desk will review your ticket and confirm the refund eligibility within 24 hours.

4. Processing Timelines

Once approved, your refund will be processed back to the original method of payment within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your financial institution's processing cycles.

5. Cancellation of Pass

Because all CedCoss TV Solution passes are purchased as one-time upfront durations (with zero hidden recurring auto-renewals), no formal cancellation request is necessary to stop future billing. When your pass expires, you may simply choose whether or not to renew.
