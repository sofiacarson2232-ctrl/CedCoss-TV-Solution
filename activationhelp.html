How Code-Based Device Activation Works

When you install an IPTV player or streaming media app on a connected TV, a temporary alphanumeric authentication code appears on screen. Entering that code here links your hardware identifier with your active CedCoss TV Solution subscription tier, eliminating the need to type lengthy passwords with a TV remote.
